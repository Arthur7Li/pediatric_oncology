# Pediatric Oncology Resources in Canada

This document outlines the premier hospitals and major financial grants available for pediatric oncology patients across Canada. It is intended to help parents and caregivers navigate the healthcare system in their region, ensuring they know where to access the best care and financial support. These guidelines align with resources vetted by institutions such as St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, SickKids, Children's Oncology Group (COG), and the National Cancer Institute (NCI).

## British Columbia

- **Hospitals**: [BC Children's Hospital](http://www.bcchildrens.ca/)
- **Financial Support**: PharmaCare, BC Childhood Cancer Parents Association (BCCCPA) Family Support Fund.
- **Notes**: Premier care provided in Vancouver, serving BC and the Yukon.

## Alberta

- **Hospitals**: Stollery Children's Hospital (Edmonton), Alberta Children's Hospital (Calgary)
- **Financial Support**: Kids with Cancer Society, Alberta Child Health Benefit, Helping Families Handle Cancer.
- **Notes**: Comprehensive care located in Edmonton and Calgary. Edmonton frequently supports Northwest Territories patients.

## Saskatchewan

- **Hospitals**: Jim Pattison Children's Hospital (Saskatoon)
- **Financial Support**: Saskatchewan Family Health Benefits, CIBC Pediatric Oncology Family Comfort Fund.
- **Notes**: Strong in-province care localized in Saskatoon.

## Manitoba

- **Hospitals**: Children's Hospital of Winnipeg (CancerCare Manitoba)
- **Financial Support**: Manitoba Pharmacare, Manitoba Candlelighters Benevolent Fund, Variety Manitoba.
- **Notes**: Primary center in Winnipeg, which also provides care for portions of Nunavut.

## Ontario

- **Hospitals**: SickKids, CHEO, McMaster Children's Hospital, Children's Hospital at London Health Sciences Centre
- **Financial Support**: OHIP+, Trillium Drug Program, POGO Financial Assistance Program.
- **Notes**: Excellent localized support with multiple world-class facilities. CHEO in Ottawa also heavily supports Nunavut patients.

## Quebec

- **Hospitals**: CHU Sainte-Justine, Montreal Children's Hospital, CHU de Québec-Université Laval
- **Financial Support**: RAMQ, Leucan.
- **Notes**: Comprehensive care and robust financial support primarily driven by Leucan.

## New Brunswick

- **Hospitals**: Dr. Georges-L.-Dumont UHC, Saint John Regional Hospital (clinics)
- **Financial Support**: New Brunswick Prescription Drug Program, Fuel the Care, Canadian Cancer Society Travel Fund.
- **Notes**: Lacks major specialized pediatric oncology center; families are typically referred to the **IWK Health Centre** in Halifax, Nova Scotia for comprehensive treatment.

## Nova Scotia

- **Hospitals**: IWK Health Centre
- **Financial Support**: Nova Scotia Family Pharmacare, IWK Foundation.
- **Notes**: The premier pediatric oncology hospital for Atlantic Canada, serving NS, NB, and PEI.

## Prince Edward Island

- **Hospitals**: Queen Elizabeth Hospital (satellite services)
- **Financial Support**: PEI Pharmacare, IWK Foundation.
- **Notes**: Lacks comprehensive specialized resources; families must travel to the **IWK Health Centre** in Halifax, Nova Scotia for most pediatric oncology care.

## Newfoundland and Labrador

- **Hospitals**: Janeway Children's Health and Rehabilitation Centre
- **Financial Support**: NLPDP, Candlelighters NL, Dr. Jack Hand Legacy Foundation.
- **Notes**: Janeway handles the majority of cases in St. John's, but highly complex cases may be referred to IWK in Halifax.

## Yukon

- **Hospitals**: Whitehorse General Hospital (limited)
- **Financial Support**: Yukon Pharmacare, Yukon Medical Travel Program.
- **Notes**: Lacks specialized pediatric oncology resources; patients are typically referred to **BC Children's Hospital** in Vancouver for specialized care.

## Northwest Territories

- **Hospitals**: Stanton Territorial Hospital (limited)
- **Financial Support**: NWT Medical Travel Service, Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB), Kids with Cancer Society.
- **Notes**: Lacks specialized pediatric oncology care; patients are sent to the **Stollery Children's Hospital** in Edmonton, Alberta.

## Nunavut

- **Hospitals**: Qikiqtani General Hospital (limited)
- **Financial Support**: Nunavut Medical Travel, Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB).
- **Notes**: Lacks specialized resources; patients are referred to **CHEO** in Ottawa or **CancerCare Manitoba** in Winnipeg depending on the region.
